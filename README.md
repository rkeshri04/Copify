# Copify
Your clipboard has a memory now.

Copify is a free, open source macOS menu bar app that saves everything you copy. It lives quietly in your menu bar and keeps a history of your last 20 copied items so you never lose something you copied. You can search through your clipboard history instantly, pin your most used snippets to keep them permanently, and access everything with one click. No subscription, no bloat, no Electron — just a tiny native Swift app that does one thing well.

## Features

- **Clipboard history** — last 20 items always saved
- **Search** — type to instantly filter your history
- **Pin** — star items to keep them forever
- **Lightweight** — native Swift, tiny app size
- **Free and open source**

Who it's for: Developers, designers, writers, and anyone who copies and pastes multiple times a day on a Mac.

## Requirements

- macOS (Intel or Apple Silicon)

3. Launch Copify from Applications or Spotlight.

## Usage

Copify runs silently in your macOS menu bar. Click the Copify icon to open the clipboard history menu.

- **Copy text**: Works normally with `Cmd + C`. Copify will automatically save it.
- **Search**: Start typing in the search bar at the top of the menu to filter both pinned and recent items.
- **Pin**: Star items to keep them forever. They will stick around across sessions.
